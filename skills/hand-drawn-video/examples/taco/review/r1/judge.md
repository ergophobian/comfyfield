  caption band never covered by the picture.

## FIX LIST (most important first)

1. **32.25–34.5** · The three bites remove only about a quarter of the dish, and bite 3 (33.75) can't be seen
   (33.15 and 33.90 look the same). The bite shape also erases the plate's rim and outline, so the plate looks
   bitten · The viewer must see three big, separate bites, each taking about a third. After 33.75 the taco is
   gone: an intact empty plate with crumbs and the lime, held to 34.5 · lifts **Clarity, Pacing, Wow** (and
   Craft: no bitten plate).
2. **0.0–3.0 and 30.0–34.5** · The "taco" is two flat discs nearly as wide as the plate. It reads as a tostada
   or pizza (the blind read says so), and it sits small in the lower half under ~40% empty paper · From frame 0
   the viewer must recognise a street taco: small tortillas, the plate rim clearly showing around them, edges
   lifted so the filling sits in a curve. The dish should be the big focal point of the square (roughly the
   middle 60% of the height) · lifts **Hook, Clarity, Craft, Composition, Wow**.
3. **3.5–6.0** · Apart labels. The leader lines run through the words (CILANTRO, PINEAPPLE, SALSA VERDE, WHITE
   ONION, PORK AL PASTOR all look struck through) and end in empty air, not on their layer. SALSA VERDE and
   CILANTRO point at the same green cloud, and salsa verde looks like green peas. PINEAPPLE sits right above the
   lime while LIME has no leader. The left CORN TORTILLA label is 28 px from the frame edge · Every word must be
   clean with its leader starting outside it. Each leader must end on its own layer. The viewer must tell the
   salsa (a sauce) from the cilantro (leaves). The lime must sit near the stack with its own leader. All labels
   need ≥40 px margin · lifts **Clarity, Readability, Craft, Composition**.
4. **0.0–6.0** · A dashed vertical guide line at x=540 (y≈240–1060) shows from frame 0 through the apart scene,
   a leftover layout guide · Only the drawing is on the page · real error; lifts **Hook, Craft**.
5. **6.0–9.0** · Kicker "~2 YEARS TO DINNER": at phone size the tilde reads as a minus, and the blind viewer read
   "-2 YEARS", negative two years · The viewer must read "about two years" at a glance, with no symbol that can
   pass for a minus · real error; lifts **Readability**.
6. **15.5–18.0** · On the map, the state names (Sinaloa, Jalisco, Michoacán, Veracruz) are ~1.4% of the height
   in dim grey, unreadable on a phone, although the caption says "Four states". The trucks are tiny, so there's
   little to follow for ~2.5 s · The viewer must read all four state names on a phone (≥2% height, high
   contrast) and watch four trucks, big enough to see, travel the routes into Mexico City. Ocean, graticule and
   scale text can stay small · lifts **Readability, Clarity**.
7. **19.3–20.9** · Night kitchen → day metate is a hard pop mid-sentence (~19.4). The grinding isn't read as
   grinding ("couldn't tell what the arm was doing"). METATE is crossed by its own leader and read as
   "META"/"METAT"; MASA is near-invisible as it fades in · The viewer must see the night pass on screen (window
   and room going from night to morning) before the stone. Then the viewer must see the kernels on one end of the
   stone turn into dough that gathers into the ball as the stone is pushed. Both labels must read whole and
   clearly against their background · lifts **Clarity, Motion, Readability**.
8. **15.0–15.4** · The ink bloom into the map is an irregular dark lump growing over the corn, read as "a boulder
   or meteor landing in the corn" · The viewer must read this moment as the scene turning into the night map,
   with no object landing in the field · lifts **Transitions, Craft**.
9. **20.9–21.4** · During the drop into the trompo, two kickers ("1 NIGHT" over "6 HOURS") and two caption bands
   are on screen at once, with the old caption half-covered. The ComfyField wordmark goes dark-on-dark for
   ~0.3 s (21.1) · Only one header shows at any moment, and the logo stays readable through the change · lifts
   **Motion, Transitions**.
10. **6.0 / 9.0 / 12.0** · Three farm scenes enter with the same leftward slide, and 12.0 adds a bright white
    vertical line on the seam that reads as a glitch · The three farm changes must look visibly different from
    each other, with no glitch line · lifts **Transitions, Motion**.
11. **34.5–36.0** · The end logo cross-fades in over the ghosted dish and caption (both visible at once) instead
    of springing in. The clean card holds only ~1 s · The viewer must see the empty plate leave, then the logo
    spring in on a clean page, with the finished card held ≥1.5 s · lifts **Pacing, Motion**.
