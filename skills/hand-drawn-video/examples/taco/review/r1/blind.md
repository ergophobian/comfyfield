## What the video is about
How a plate of tacos al pastor ("this taco") comes together. It gets pulled apart, and each ingredient gets a short scene with a countdown tag: pineapple (2 years), pig (6 months), corn (4 months), trucks (2 days), soaking corn (1 night), pork on a spit (6 hours), tortillas on a pan (1 minute), then shaving the pork (NOW). It ends on eating at 8:02 PM: "Two years to grow. Three bites to eat." Then a brand end card.

## What I'd remember
- The pineapple growing from a tiny plant to a ripe fruit, and the piglet growing into a big pig.
- The tall spit of meat with a pineapple on top, and the knife flicking a chunk of pineapple.
- The closing line "Two years to grow. Three bites to eat." with the bitten dish.
- The countdown tags (-2 YEARS, 6 MONTHS, 4 MONTHS, 2 DAYS, 1 NIGHT, 6 HOURS, 1 MINUTE, NOW, 8:02 PM). I read them as a clock running toward dinner.
