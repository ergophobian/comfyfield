- Arms and hands are still flat tubes with mitten hands (19.5–21.0 grinding, 27–30 shave), and the mano is drawn as
  an upright board. A wrist, fingers and a mano lying across the stone would lift the craft.
- The comal (24.5–27) reads as "a wok on a barbecue" until COMAL appears. A flatter, shallower griddle would read
  at once.
- The bite edge is a straight vertical line of scallops (32.4–33.7) and reads as pinking shears; an arc of
  tooth marks would read as a bite.
- The pineapple flick (28.75–29.25) is a ~50 px cube. A bigger slice with a spin or a trail would sell the
  taquero's move.
- The countdown kicker is the smallest type on screen (~20 px caps, thin red), and it is the film's clock. A bolder
  or larger kicker would read better on a phone.
- Apart tortillas are pancake-thick (4.5–6.0). The pig grows by uniform scaling, and its snout overlaps the
  cactus at 11.5. The farmer mostly holds one pose (12.5–14.5).
- The map's CORN / PORK / LIMES / PINEAPPLE tags are tiny, and "MEXICO CITY" crowds "PINEAPPLE Veracruz".
- Headers are still empty for ~0.4–0.6 s at 21.0, 27.0 and 30.0 (kicker first, caption after).
- The blind viewer wondered where salsa, cilantro, onion and lime come from, and what the "street corner" is until
