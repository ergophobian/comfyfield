## What the video is about

How one street taco (al pastor with pineapple, cilantro, onion, salsa, lime, corn tortilla) is made. Each ingredient's journey is shown on a countdown clock that runs from "2 YEARS TO DINNER" down to "8:02 PM": a pineapple growing for two years, a piglet growing up, corn growing and drying, trucks converging on Mexico City, corn soaked and ground to masa, pork stacked on a spit, tortillas puffing on a comal, shaving the meat. The ending lands on "Two years to grow. Three bites to eat." and the taco is eaten in three bites, then the ComfyField logo.

## What I would remember

- The countdown tags (2 years, 6 months, 4 months, 2 days, 1 night, 6 hours, 1 minute, 8:02 PM). It gives the video a shape.
- The pineapple going from plant to golden fruit and the whole field ripening.
- The pig growing in four steps.
- The blue night map with trucks funnelling into Mexico City.
- The fire-backed spit with the pineapple on top.
- The last line, "Two years to grow. Three bites to eat.", and the taco vanishing in three bites.
- The "Drawn in code. Scored in code." tagline, as a statement of how it was made.

## Where I got lost or bored

- 0.5 to 2.5 s: a taco that barely moves (steam only) while a title types. 2 s of nothing new is the slowest stretch.
- The exploded view labels five things (salsa verde, cilantro, white onion, lime, plus tortillas, pork, pineapple) but only pineapple, pork and corn/tortilla get a story. I never saw where the salsa, cilantro, onion or lime came from, apart from a "LIMES / Michoacán" dot on the map. I expected each one.
- "One street corner" (17.0): I have no idea what the corner is until the night street scene with the spit; the map ends on Mexico City, not a corner.
- The pig: I see a pig get big, and later a cone of red meat, but nothing connects them except the caption "Pork". The same for the corn and masa.
- Order jump: the pig (6 months) and corn (4 months) come after the pineapple (2 years), fine for a countdown, but 6 hours then 1 minute then "NOW" then "8:02 PM" is a lot of tags in the last 15 s.
- 29.0 to 29.5: "Pineapple flies." I caught only a tiny yellow chunk on the tortilla at 29.0. At this sampling the flying moment is too small and short to read as the joke.
- The end card holds for about 2.5 s with nothing moving (35.5 to 37.0 and beyond).
