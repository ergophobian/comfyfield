## What the video is about

A countdown through how a taco al pastor came to be: pineapple (2 years), pig (6 months), corn (4 months), trucks to Mexico City (2 days), corn soak (1 night), pork on a spit (6 hours), tortilla on the comal (1 minute), shaved now, eaten at 8:02 PM. Closing line: "Two years to grow. Three bites to eat." Branded ComfyField, "Drawn in code. Scored in code."

## What I would remember

- The opening taco coming apart into labeled floating ingredients.
- The pineapple growing and turning gold in the field.
- The pig growing up in the farmyard.
- The big fire-lit spit with the pineapple on top.
- The taco being eaten in three bites and the empty plate, then the logo.
- The red time tags that count down from "2 YEARS" to "8:02 PM."

## Where I got lost or bored

- 5.0 to 5.5 and 2.0 to 2.5 are still holds (at 0.5 s sampling I cannot see small motion).
- Trucks scene, 15.5 to 17.5: trucks pile up and hide the Mexico City label. "Four states. One street corner." names states I cannot match to anything: I count four markers (Sinaloa, Jalisco, Michoacan, Veracruz) but the "street corner" is never shown.
- 33.0 to 34.5: the empty plate sits for about 1.5 s, then a blank cream beat before the logo. It is a long quiet tail.

- The time tags are tiny, and "6 MONTHS" for the pig and "4 MONTHS" for corn only make sense after you notice the countdown pattern. First tag, "2 YEARS TO DINNER", is the only one that says what it counts to.
- Pig reaches a huge size compared with the house; it is slightly odd, not wrong.

## Looked broken or unfinished

1. Titles are clipped mid-word at the start of nearly every scene ("How this t", "Let'", "A pineapp", "A piglet g", "Corn grows tall, then drie", etc.). It looks like a typewriter reveal, so it may be intentional, but on frame 0 it reads as a bug: the first frame shows "How this t".
2. At 6.0, 21.0 and 27.0 the title area is empty for a moment after a scene starts, while the tag is already present.
3. During side wipes (9.0, 12.0, 24.0) the old title text stays visible at the edge, cut off ("about two years to grow.", "ows up on a farm in Jalisco.", "ork in chiles and achiote, stack"), and the new tag collides with the logo ("6 MONT", "1 MINU").
4. Wipe direction is inconsistent: horizontal from the right (9, 12, 19.5, 24), from the top at 15.0, from below at 18.0 and 30.0. At 18.0 the map looks cropped/shifted mid-wipe.
5. 3.0 to 3.5: the taco opens, then the plate vanishes and the tortilla jumps lower and to the left. The jump is abrupt.
6. 4.5: the lime wedge sits over the "PINEAPPLE" label and garbles it.
7. 16.5: the truck cluster covers the "MEXICO CITY" label.
8. 19.0 to 19.5: the kitchen goes from the pot to a different table in one wipe; the arms in white sleeves have no bodies and reach in from the frame edges (also 27.5 to 29.5). They look like floating limbs.
9. 28.5: the knife looks sword-long. The "pineapple flies" moment is just a yellow chunk appearing near the tortilla; I cannot see it fly at 0.5 s sampling.
10. 11.5: the chicken disappears behind the pig (probably just covered).
11. 34.5: a tiny blue dot on a blank screen reads like an artifact before it turns into the logo.
12. The end card background (mottled cream) differs a bit from the flat cream of the plate scene before it. A small look change, not a bug.

Nothing looked like the wrong object. The pig, corn, pineapple, trucks, pot and spit all read correctly. The tiny white onion bits and green salsa line on the taco are the least obvious items without their labels.
