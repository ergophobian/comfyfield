# Music brief — "Sesquiáltera for a taco"

**Signature:** a son jarocho hemiola — 6/8 bars alternating with 3/4 feels — in D Dorian, played by a kitchen:
tortilla pats, knife chops, a comal's sizzle, a spatula's FM ting, a nylon-string strum, a wooden bar, a round bass.
A night section borrows Bb Lydian (Bbmaj7 – Am7 – Gm9 – Asus); the end opens to D Lydian.

**Tempo:** dotted-quarter 80 (eighth = 0.25 s, bar = 1.5 s) — the film's bars. Cuts sit on bar lines.

**Form = the story:** intro (taco, 0–6 s: pluck alone; toppings and lifting layers ring their own notes, pitch rising
with height) → countdown/growing (6–15: groove in, a wooden line climbing a step a bar like the plants) → night
(15–21: drops to glass pad, a tine tune, ticking like the trucks and the clock) → fire (21–30: fullest, thump on
the big beats, scrapes, the breath tune) → dinner (30–36: stop-time on the three bites, then the D Lydian chord on
the end card).

**Sync:** the score reads `K.film.cues`; every hit gets a sound by what it is (lands → wooden note + pop; puff →
pop + bell; flies → whoosh + tine; arrives → bell; grind/shave → chop + scrape); every scene change gets its
transition sound (paper for slides/turns, whoosh for push/drop, a swell into the ink wipe) and a woodblock tick —
the countdown's clock.

**Avoid:** ukulele/glockenspiel/whistling/claps; I–V–vi–IV; four-on-the-floor; mariachi trumpets or any tourist
"Mexican" cliché; lo-fi crackle. Reference music (measured) is 4/4 G major ~91 bpm — this score shares only its
warmth and slow build.
