
### Instruments
* **Mallet percussion / Vibraphone:** The prominent melodic driver carrying the bright, percussive chime and bouncy chord figures.
* **Acoustic Upright Bass:** Plucked, deep, and syncopated, providing a warm, walking bounce.
* **Percussion & Kit:** Crisp woodblocks/claves, shaker, tight rimshots/snare clicks, subtle kick, and handclaps.
* **Muted Horns / Melodica-style Keys:** Playful mid-register melodic counter-phrases and jaunty fills.
* **Warm Acoustic Piano / Rhodes:** Soft, comping harmonic support tucked underneath the mallets.

---

### Groove and Metre
* **Metre:** Standard **4/4** time.
* **Groove:** A lighthearted, syncopated mid-tempo bounce with a slight swing/reggae-calypso-funk lilt. The emphasis falls on the off-beats and sharp rim-clicks over a swaggering walking bassline, creating a steady, head-nodding stride.

---

### Mood & The Ending
* **Mood:** Whimsical, mischievous, and curious. It feels like the soundtrack to a clever indie animated short, a lighthearted heist/caper, or a playful artisan montage.
* **How the Ending Feels:** Deliberately understated and cheeky. Rather than swelling into an epic crescendo, it strips back abruptly to raw percussion, delivers one final lighthearted chime phrase around **0:34**, and decays into an open, humorous silence that leaves you smiling.

### Is it Generic Stock Music?
**No, not in the typical sense.** 
* It completely avoids standard royalty-free stock clichés: there are no cheerful strummed ukuleles, whistling hooks, glockenspiel motivational builds, or corporate tech synthesizers.
* While it clearly functions as production/cues music (suitable for comedy, games, or character animation), it uses organic acoustic textures, dynamic arrangement shifts, syncopated human swing, and deliberate negative space, giving it distinct personality rather than sounding like disposable corporate background filler.
