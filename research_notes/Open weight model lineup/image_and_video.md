# Open-weight image and video model lineup for ComfyField (as of 2026-10-16)

Method note: every claim below was checked on a live page on 2026-10-16. Sources are linked inline. Licences were read from the Hugging Face API (`huggingface.co/api/models/<id>`, `cardData.license`) or the LICENSE file itself. "Native" means ComfyUI core nodes plus an official template in Comfy's template index ([templates/index.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/index.json)); template "usage" counts in that file are used as a popularity signal. The web-search quota ran out mid-research, so later checks used direct fetches of known pages; some 2026 releases may be missing (see Gaps). arena.ai (formerly LMArena) returned 403 to every fetch, so LMArena ranks are unverified. Anything not confirmed is marked **unverified**.

## Takeaway

Open-weight quality is now close to the closed frontier, but licences, not quality, decide most picks. MiniMax H3 is the best open video model (Artificial Analysis image-to-video #1 overall at 1141, text-to-video 1159, video editing #4 and the only open entry), it is native in ComfyUI and preinstalled on Comfy Cloud, but its community licence excludes the US, EU, UK and South Korea: US users are covered only on Comfy Cloud (generations there include commercial rights) or with a local licence that Comfy resells from $5,000/month. LTX-2.5 is open (22B, 2026-08-11), fast, has native multi-shot, first/last frame, joint audio and a large IC-LoRA toolkit (clean plate, alpha matte, day-to-night, restore, HDR), but ranks far lower (about 950 vs 1159) and its licence forbids use in a product that "directly competes" with Lightricks without a separate licence. FLUX 3 itself (video and image) is API-only and FLUX 3 Dev has no date, but BFL released FLUX 3 Image Klein yesterday (5.6B, open, free commercial use under $5M revenue, native in ComfyUI with Cloud templates), which is the only open-weight way to meet the contest's "FLUX3 must be the core model" bonus. The best open image model on the leaderboards, Qwen-Image-2.1, is research-only (non-commercial), so the commercially safe image defaults are FLUX 3 Image Klein, Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Apache 2.0) and HiDream-O1 / Ming-Image (MIT). Wan 2.2 remains Alibaba's newest open base video model (Wan 2.5 to 3.0 are API-only), and the cleanest-licensed video stack is Wan 2.2 plus its 2026 add-ons (Wan Animate 2, Bernini-R, SCAIL-2, InfiniteTalk), SeedVR2, RIFE, SAM 3.1 and BiRefNet.

## Key surprises

1. **MiniMax H3's licence excludes the US, EU, UK and South Korea.** The best open video model cannot be self-hosted in the US without a licence that Comfy resells (from $5,000/month); on Comfy Cloud, commercial rights are included. For a US Runpod path, H3 is off the table ([LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/LICENSE), [Comfy licence](https://comfy.org/minimax/license/)).
2. **LTX-2.x forbids use in a product that directly competes with Lightricks** without a separate licence (Attachment A item 20), on top of the $10M revenue cap ([LICENSE-2_x](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/main/LICENSE-2_x)).
3. **FLUX 3 is API-only, except FLUX 3 Image Klein, released 2026-10-15**, one day before this research. FLUX 3 Dev (the promised open video and image backbone) still has no date ([BFL Klein](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein), [FLUX 3 Video](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-video)).
4. **The top open image model, Qwen-Image-2.1, is research-only**, unlike every earlier Qwen-Image release (Apache 2.0). Comfy Cloud's "every model is cleared for commercial use" statement conflicts with this licence; treat Qwen-Image-2.1 as non-commercial until Comfy or Qwen says otherwise ([LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1/blob/main/LICENSE), [comfy.org/cloud](https://www.comfy.org/cloud)).
5. **Ideogram 4.0 has open weights** (non-commercial) and is the #2 open text-to-image model ([Comfy-Org/Ideogram-4](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Ideogram-4)).
6. **Every Wan after 2.2 is closed** (2.5, 2.6, 2.7, 3.0), yet Alibaba keeps shipping open Wan 2.2 add-ons: Wan Animate 2 (2026-08) and Wan Dancer ([HF Wan-AI](https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=Wan-AI)).
7. **LTX-2.5 "Fast" and "Pro" are hosted API tiers**, not checkpoints; the open weights are dev and distilled. Likewise **"H3 Max" and H3 2K output are hosted only**; open H3 is 768p ([LTX API docs](https://docs.ltx.io/models/ltx-2-5), [H3 card](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3)).
8. **LTX-2.5 now has official IC-LoRAs for several Higgsfield-style effects**: clean plate (remove people), alpha matte, day-to-night relight, restore, colorize, SDR to HDR ([Lightricks HF](https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=Lightricks&search=LTX-2.5)).
9. **The contest's "Open Weights Only" prize bans partner nodes and APIs**, so FLUX 3 Video, H3 Max, Router calls and Wan 3.0 would disqualify an entry from that prize ([contest post](https://blog.comfy.org/p/higgsfield-promised-to-open-source)).
