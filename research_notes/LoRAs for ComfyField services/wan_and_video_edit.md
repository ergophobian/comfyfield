# Wan-family and video-edit-model LoRAs for ComfyField services (as of 2026-10-19)

How to read this file:
- Every popularity number was fetched on 2026-10-19. On Hugging Face (HF), `dl30` is downloads in the last 30 days and `dlAll` is all-time downloads, both from the HF API (`expand[]=downloadsAllTime`). On Civitai, `dl` is all-time downloads and `up` is thumbs-up count, from `civitai.com/api/v1/models/<id>`.
- "Base" is the base model exactly as the page states it: the Civitai `baseModel` string (for example `Wan Video 2.2 I2V-A14B`, `Wan Video 14B i2v 480p`, or the generic `Wan Video`) or the HF `base_model` field.
- License: HF license tag, or the Civitai permission flags (`Image` = sell generated images, `Rent` = use on other generation services, `RentCivit` = Civitai generation only, `Sell` = sell the model). Licenses are not a reason to drop a pick (review after the 2026-10-30 submission).
- "HN" = high-noise expert, "LN" = low-noise expert of Wan 2.2 A14B. Sizes are per file as Civitai or HF shows them.
- Excluded, and not listed: NSFW-focused LoRAs (including one Bernini-R "motion enhancer" pack whose repo also ships anatomy LoRAs and recommends an NSFW text encoder), LoRAs of real identifiable people, kiss LoRAs, and style LoRAs named after living artists or directors (several anime-director and studio-named styles, and one pixel-art style named after a living artist).
- Method: HF adapter trees (`filter=base_model:adapter:<repo>`, sorted by downloads, 26 base repos), the Civitai API per base model and per keyword, then each pick's own HF card or Civitai page. Raw pulls are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.

## Q1. Which Wan effect LoRAs are most used, and which ComfyField and Higgsfield-style Effects do they cover?

### Takeaway
The most-used open effect LoRAs are still the Remade-AI Wan 2.1 I2V 480p set from March to May 2025 (360 rotation 176k HF downloads, squish, inflate, cakeify, crush, muscle), plus a large Wan 2.2 I2V A14B wave from Aug to Dec 2025 (hxxwoq2222, artificialguybr "Redmond", luisa_caotica, _GhostInShell_, kabachuha). About 38 of ComfyField's 166 Effects formats have a direct Wan LoRA and about 37 more have a partial match. The classic viral set (squish, inflate, cakeify, crush, deflate, muscle, 360 spin) has ready LoRAs, but none of these is in ComfyField's current Effects list.

### Inferences
- Name-level mapping of ComfyField's 166 Effects (my mapping, from the tables above): direct Wan LoRA for about 38 formats — Turn to sand, Time lapse, Burst into petals, High flip, Eyes in, Street giant, Turn to ice, Building explosion, Car explosion, Fire breath, Invisible body, Melting, Ripple morph, Set on fire, Lightning eyes, Chrome body, Headwind, Overgrown, Blast to dust, Invisible float, Action run, Moonwalk (x3), Burning figure, Power up fighter, Frozen moment, Puff of smoke, Melt, City giant, Claymation, Dive into the eye, Giant in the city, Exploded building, Wax melt, Frozen diner orbit, Poof only clothes, Pixel dissolve. Partial for about 37 (Glitch out, Studio slide, World morph, Paint splash, Flower face, Glow change, Paparazzi, Levitation, Powder burst, Storm rise, Storm metal, Molten metal, Pearl portrait, Winged painting, Living portrait, Painting mirror, Everyone freezes, Frozen fashion pose, Frozen mid-leap, Desert worms, Lightning monster, Titan double, Doodle melt, Suited agent, Stage punch, Boxing combo, High kick, Breakneck ride, Stadium star, Orbit to street, Studio cross-wipe, Two scenes one crane, Photocopy glitch aisle, Flood, Shatter, 70s thriller, Floating fall). About 91 have no LoRA (Snow globe, Portal, Hologram, Angel wings, Tentacles, Clones, Scrapbook page, Pigeon street, most of the 45 "Turn your references into..." set).
- The classic Pika/Higgsfield-era set (squish, inflate, cakeify, crush, deflate, muscle, 360 spin) is the cheapest win: ready, popular, permissively licensed LoRAs, and ComfyField does not offer these formats yet.
- Most Wan 2.2 effect LoRAs are HN-only; only the HN expert needs the effect LoRA, so one LoRA file per effect is enough.

### Gaps
- No public A/B test was found that runs the Remade Wan 2.1 480p LoRAs on Wan 2.2 A14B; compatibility on 2.2 rests on community reports (see Q6).
- No open LoRA found for: Zero gravity, Hug (non-kiss couple hug), Snow globe, Portal, Hologram, Angel wings, Clones / Endless clones, Tentacles, Paparazzi flashes, Scrapbook page, Origami fold (as a transformation), Pigeons, Monster dab.
- Civitai's unauthenticated API returns only SFW-visible models (173 items for `Wan Video 2.2 I2V-A14B`); popularity ranks therefore cover the SFW subset only.

## Q4. Which speed LoRAs exist (lightx2v Lightning / distill, CausVid, FusionX-style merges), and how do they change quality?

### Takeaway
The standard is lightx2v's 4-step distill LoRA pair for Wan 2.2 A14B (10.9M all-time downloads), now in a 2026-04-12 (260412) version; ComfyUI's own templates ship the older v1 pair. Quality is close to base in most scenes, but the default pair is known for slow motion and artifacts in very large motion; FusionX (CausVid + AccVid + MoviiGen + MPS merge) is faster and punchier but carries non-commercial components and causes color shift and identity loss over long InfiniteTalk clips.

## Q6. How are these LoRAs loaded in ComfyUI, and what prompt pattern do they need?

### Takeaway
All picks except BFS head swap, Bernini-R-S2V, EverAnimate and Stand-In load with the standard model-only LoRA loader. On Wan 2.2 A14B (and Bernini-R) each expert has its own LoRA slot: the speed LoRA goes on both, most effect and camera LoRAs go on the high-noise expert only, and a few style or texture LoRAs go on the low-noise expert. Prompts are "trigger + plain description of the action".

### Cited Findings
- Wan 2.2 A14B is a Mixture-of-Experts model that splits denoising "across timesteps with specialized powerful expert models" — [Wan2.2 README](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2).

## Q7. Strategic: would moving the Effects formats from LTX to Wan 2.2 + effect LoRAs match Higgsfield's effects better?

### Takeaway
Yes for a defined subset. Wan has ready LoRAs for about 38 of the 166 Effects (plus about 37 partial), while the LTX side has about 9 direct matches spread across four incompatible LTX generations and none confirmed for the "LTX Video 2B" base that 143 Effects use today. ### Inferences
- Evidence count (my name-level mapping from Q1): Wan direct ≈ 38 / 166 (23%), Wan partial ≈ 37, none ≈ 91. LTX direct ≈ 9 (Melting, Melt, Wax melt, Building explosion, Exploded building, Car explosion, Turn to sand, Blast to dust, Pixel dissolve; plus partial Levitation, Paper puppet, Claymation, Studio cross-wipe), and these sit on LTXV 13B, LTX-2 19B and LTX-2.3 22B, which do not share LoRA weights with each other or with a 2B model.
- Match to Higgsfield: Higgsfield presets are fixed recipes on a photo; a trained effect LoRA reproduces a fixed motion recipe far more reliably than a prompt on a small 2B model. So for the 38 matched effects, Wan 2.2 + LoRA should match Higgsfield better. For the 91 unmatched ones, Wan has no advantage over a stronger prompt-only base.
- Recommended split:
  1. Wan 2.2 I2V A14B + lightx2v 4-step + effect LoRA: the 38 direct matches, plus new classic formats (Squish, Inflate, Cakeify, Crush, Deflate, Muscle, 360 spin) using Remade or the Wan 2.2 re-trains (kabachuha squish, Elix_LX cake, hxxwoq inflate).
  2. Prompt-only on MiniMax H3 (top open I2V per the lineup notes) or LTX-2.5 for the rest; drop "LTX Video 2B" as the default effect base regardless.
  3. Later: train custom Wan 2.2 HN LoRAs (30–50 clips each, AI Toolkit or musubi, as the authors above did) for the top unmatched Higgsfield effects (Clones, Portal, Angel wings, Snow globe, Zero gravity, Hug).
- Risks: Remade LoRAs are Wan 2.1 480p (test on 2.2 or run them on Wan 2.1 I2V 480p); many Civitai picks lack the `Image` or derivative flags (hxxwoq2222, color2023, _GhostInShell_), which the license review must check; cost per clip of a 14B MoE at 4–8 steps vs a 2B model.

- "LTX Video 2B" in the inventory may be a label; if it actually means LTX-2 (19B), the LTX-2 LoRAs above become directly usable.
