From one line to a scheduled post

  1. Insert your productWrite one line: what you sell and who it is for. Add a product photo or a character if you have one.
  2. Pick a formatThe format's writer layers its proven structure on top of your line. You read and edit the plan before anything renders.
  3. Approve the cheap frame, then renderEach video makes a still with Flux 2 Pro first and waits for you. Only an approved frame becomes a Seedance 2.5 clip, with sound.
  4. Queue and scheduleEvery output lands in the Library. Keep an idea queue per channel and schedule posts to your channels with Postiz.

Longer stories go to Cinema

Cinema plans 2 to 6 shots from one idea, renders each with LTX-2.5 on Comfy Cloud, and lets you approve or rerun shot by shot before joining them. This scene was made that way.

Let your agent run it

Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client can fill each channel's idea queue, write and generate, run flows, and schedule the posts you approve. Flows stop at every approval gate. Only you can press Send.

Start in three steps

  1. Install Node.js and GitGet Node.js 22 or newer and Git, then reopen your terminal.
  2. Paste the three linesnpm start installs everything, builds the studio and opens it in your browser. The first start takes a few minutes.
  3. Add your Comfy keyOpen Connections, paste a key from platform.comfy.org and choose Save and test.

You pay only your own Comfy credits, nothing to ComfyField, and you approve the frame before any paid video renders.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/ergophobian/comfyfield.git
cd comfyfield
