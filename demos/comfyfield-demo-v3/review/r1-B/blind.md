## What it is about
An ad for ComfyField, an open-source, MIT-licensed desktop tool for making AI videos. It runs on your computer with your own API keys and gathers many video and image tools and models (LTX-2.5, Wan 2.2, MiniMax H3) in one place. It claims 22 proven short-video formats and the ability to make motion graphics by code with no image model. It is built on the Comfy platform.

## What I would remember
- The opening burst into a huge wall of thumbnails, and the "Every tool. One field." line.
- The slot-machine model names that collapse into an "LTX-2.5" button, then a cinematic purple-portal shot.
- The paper-style taco and pineapple drawings, a "how did they do that?" moment, especially with the claim "No image or video model", meaning they were drawn in code.
- The thumbnails swirling into the logo at the end.

## Where I got lost or bored
- The "Cinema" clip holds for about 2 s with almost no motion, and its caption is tiny.
- 17 s jumps to a phone with a gym woman. Nothing explains why that follows "your own keys".
- "Formats that earn" is abstract. I could not tell what "earn" means (money? views?). I saw the chips but could not read most of them.
- The Custom skills section (taco) comes after the formats section with no link. It is charming but I did not understand how it connects to the product, and the cards change fast.
- "Open-source video models, at your hand." reads awkwardly (grammar).

## Could not read
- Most chips around the phone (tiny).
- The "Cinema" subtitle line.
- The pink pill at 19 to 19.5 s.
- Text on the Mexico map card and the card labels in the taco section.
- The bottom credit "Made with Seedance 2.5 by the ComfyField team."
- The pills on the end card are tiny, and "Built on the Comfy Developer Platform" is hard to read while it appears.
