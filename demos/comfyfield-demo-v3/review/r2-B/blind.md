## What it is about
A promo for ComfyField, an open-source app that runs on your own computer with your own API keys and puts many video/AI tools and models (LTX, Wan, MiniMax, Seedance) in one place, with ready-made "formats" for short vertical videos. The paper section claims it also has agent skills that draw motion graphics in code with no image or video model.

## Memorable / "how did they do that?"
- The giant 3D thumbnail floor and the burst into it.
- The slot-reel model picker, settling onto LTX-2.5, then the portal "Cinema" shot.
- The paper-card taco film: told it was made purely in code, which is impressive if true.
- The vortex of cards collapsing into the logo.

## Lost or bored
- Roughly 7-11 s: the reels are busy; I didn't know why models were being "picked", and "at your hand" is odd phrasing.
- 16-17.5 s: "Every tool on your computer, with your own keys" shows for a short time over busy blobs; it is the key claim but easy to miss.
- The jump from phone/"Formats that earn" to the beige paper section is abrupt; it is not clear the paper section is a different feature (skills) until you read the text.
- It never shows the actual app interface (buttons, timeline), so I can't tell how I'd use it.
- Meaning of "Formats that earn" (earn what?) is unclear.

## Couldn't read
- Tiny chips ("Relight", "Lipsync", "Try-on", "Motion control") at contact-sheet size.
- Phone-section chip labels, the "Made with Seedance 2.5" credit line, and paper-card sub captions (e.g. "A pineapple takes about two years to grow").
- The bottom pills and the github URL are small.
- Text in the vortex frames.
